Where your budget actually lands

21 of the 35 live-checked properties cost under ₹1,000 per room per night. Another 14 land between ₹1,000 and ₹1,313 — still inside your ceiling. None of the live-checked budget list needed your ₹2,000 stretch price. For 4 adults in 2 rooms for 3 nights you are realistically looking at ₹3,100 – ₹7,900 plus taxes depending on how much comfort you want.

How to read the area tiers

Vizag is long and thin, hugging the coast. Almost everything a visitor wants to see (RK Beach, Kailasagiri, Rushikonda, the submarine museum) sits on the Beach Road / Rushikonda axis, while the railway station and the old commercial core sit inland at Daba Gardens / Jagadamba / Allipuram. Gajuwaka and the outer wards are far from both and only make sense if you are driving through or working there.

Distances are road distances to Visakhapatnam Railway Station unless stated, taken from Goibibo's own landmark table for each property. They are approximate.

Suggested booking plan

1. Book HOTEL MANTHRALAYA or Shyam Inn first if you want the absolute cheapest. Both are in Railway New Colony, both have parking, and both came in at ₹522–₹600 per room per night on the live check. Two rooms for three nights is roughly ₹3,600–₹4,000 including taxes.

2. If you want the beach within walking distance, take The Royal Inn (MVP Colony, ₹1,169/room, parking, 09054205593) or CHARVI GUEST HOUSE (₹700/room, parking). Both put you on the Beach Road side without breaking budget.

3. If you want the best-reviewed cheap room, call HOTEL MARINA GRAND on 07041543905 — 4.9/5 from 916 ratings, parking, breakfast, near the station.

4. Book the parking ones by phone where a number exists. For the rest, Goibibo/OYO listings are the fastest route, and OYO/Treebo/FabHotels publish their own central booking lines.

5. Nothing here needed your ₹2,000 stretch. If you do spend it, spend it on a sea-view room on Beach Road rather than on a bigger number in Gajuwaka.

Method and limits

  • Live prices were read from Goibibo on 28 Sep 2026 using a search set to 18–21 Oct 2026, 2 rooms, 4 adults, Visakhapatnam city. Those prices are shown as “for 2 rooms per night” exactly as the site presented them, and exclude taxes unless the tax line is given.
  • Justdial was used for phone numbers, area-level addresses and the “Parking Available” badges. Justdial per-night figures are the site's published indicative rates, not dated quotes — they are labelled as such everywhere they appear.
  • Full postal addresses and parking amenities for the shortlisted properties were pulled from Goibibo's own address pages for each hotel.
  • APTDC (tourism.ap.gov.in) is the official AP state booking channel. On 28 Sep 2026 its pricing API returned authorisation errors, so no dated rate could be obtained for the two Visakhapatnam Haritha properties. The live booking flow was confirmed to exist.
  • What I did not do: invent or infer any phone number, address or rate. Where a number is behind Justdial's “Show Number” button, or could only be found in a social-media post, that is said plainly on the card.
  • Rates move. Three nights, four adults and a festival-free midweek window are in your favour, but re-check before you pay.
